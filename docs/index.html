What it does

Transcription is the easy part.
The product is the cleanup.

Hold CapsLock to talk, release to insert â€” in Word, Outlook, Slack, VS Code, any browser field. Double-tap for hands-free. Shift+CapsLock keeps normal caps.

Privacy

Voice never leaves the machine

Speech recognition runs on-device with whisper.cpp. Only the resulting text is sent for cleanup â€” and there's a fully offline fallback.

Intelligence

A fast Claude pass strips fillers, punctuates, and resolves self-corrections: “Tuesday â€” wait, no, Friday” becomes just “Friday.”

Editing

Voice commands on selected text

Select a sentence, hold the key, say “make this more concise.” The selection is rewritten in place.

Context

Knows where it's typing

Formal register in Outlook, casual in Slack â€” a configurable per-app tone map. Plus a custom dictionary for names and jargon.

Trust

Silence gates keep hallucinated text out of your documents. API down? It degrades to offline cleanup instead of breaking.
